Estrella Music is a powerful, elegant, and modern music player designed for high-quality audio playback and library management.

Key Features:
- Local-First Music Player: Seamlessly scans, indexes, and plays your local audio files (MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG, OPUS).
- Rich Metadata & ID3 Tag Extraction: Automatic artwork and tag display for tracks, albums, and artists.
- Synced & Plain Lyrics: Sing along with real-time synchronized and translated lyrics.
- Smart Audio Engine: Built-in equalizer, sleep timer, silence skipping, and smooth gapless playback.
- Offline Downloads & Smart Caching: Keep your favorite tracks available on your device.
- Multi-Profile & External Streaming: Configure your personal music streaming server and profiles.
- Clean & Customizable UI: Dynamic Material You themes, bottom or side navigation bar, and dark mode support.
- Android Auto Support: Enjoy your music safely while driving.
- Privacy & Ad-Free: Pure music experience with no advertisements.
